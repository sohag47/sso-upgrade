# Business Rules

## Purpose
This document records confirmed application-specific business rules that affect workflows, permissions, validation, and data handling.

## Source of Truth
- Record rules only when confirmed by the user, product requirements, approved documentation, or verified backend behavior.
- Do not infer business rules from UI labels or guess based on common industry practice.
- When a rule is unclear or conflicting, ask for clarification.

## Rule Entry Template
For each rule, document:
- **ID:** Stable identifier, such as `BR-001`.
- **Name:** Short descriptive title.
- **Description:** Exact expected behavior.
- **Applies to:** Feature, role, or workflow.
- **Validation:** Conditions that must be satisfied.
- **Source:** Requirement, API contract, or user confirmation.
- **Status:** Confirmed, proposed, or deprecated.
